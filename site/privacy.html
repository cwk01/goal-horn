Goal Horn

Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy covers the Goal Horn app for Android and the Goal Horn desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux, including the Docker version. Goal Horn is made by Colby Kordas.

Goal Horn doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your device

Your team, settings, TV delay, goal list and any MP3s you choose are saved on your phone or computer only. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Who the app talks to

To show scores and play horns, the app downloads public data directly from these services:

  • NHL (api-web.nhle.com, assets.nhle.com) for NHL and AHL scores and logos
  • HockeyTech (lscluster.hockeytech.com, assets.leaguestat.com) for AHL and PWHL scores
  • MLB (statsapi.mlb.com, mlbstatic.com) for baseball scores and logos
  • ESPN (site.api.espn.com, espncdn.com) for football scores and logos
  • goalhorn.colbykordas.com, hosted on Cloudflare, for team horn sounds

These requests carry nothing about you beyond what every web request does: your IP address and basic device information, which those services may log under their own privacy policies. Goal Horn doesn't keep logs of horn downloads.

Your Wi-Fi network

When you use Find computers on Android, the app checks addresses on your own Wi-Fi network for a Goal Horn computer. That traffic stays on your network. The desktop app shares the game, your team and the horn with phones and browsers on the same network so they can play along.

Permissions

The Android app uses internet access, network and Wi-Fi state (to find a Goal Horn computer), audio settings (to play the horn) and vibration. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone. When you pick your own MP3, Android's file picker gives the app that one file only.

Children

Goal Horn isn't directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The app's source code is public at github.com/cwk01/goal-horn, so you can check what it does.
