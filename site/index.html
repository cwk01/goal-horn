Your team scores. The whole house hears it.

Goal Horn is a free app that plays your team's horn the moment they score, on your computer and on every phone, tablet and TV in the house.

Download for Windows Download for Mac Free and open source. Also on Linux and Raspberry Pi.
Press it. This is the horn Goal Horn ships with.

Set up in about two minutes

  1. Install and pick your team

    Download the app, open it, and choose who you cheer for. It finds their next game on its own.

  2. Add speakers

    Scan the code in the app with a phone or tablet, or open the address on a smart TV. Tap "Turn on sound" once.

  3. Watch the game

    When your team scores, every speaker plays the horn. Set the TV delay so it goes off when you see the goal, not before.

Questions

Do I need to install anything on the phones and TVs?

No. They use their web browser. Only the computer running Goal Horn needs the app, and every device has to be on the same Wi-Fi.

Will it go off before I see the goal on TV?

Not once the TV delay is set. Cable runs about 30 seconds behind live and streaming apps can run 45 to 90 seconds behind. Goal Horn holds the scoreboard and the horn back by the amount you choose.

Windows or my Mac says the app might not be safe.

Goal Horn isn't signed with a paid developer certificate yet, so you see this warning once. On Windows click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway. Windows will then ask about network access: allow it, or speakers can't connect.

Can I use my own horn or song?

Yes. Upload any MP3 in Settings and it plays for that team. Your file stays on your computer.

Does my computer have to stay on?

Yes, during the game. Closing the window keeps Goal Horn running in the tray or menu bar, and it keeps the computer from sleeping while a game is live.

Which sports play the horn automatically?

Goals in the NHL, AHL and PWHL, runs in MLB, and touchdowns in the NFL. The win horn plays when your team wins. You can also press the button whenever you like.
